How to Calculate Percentage? – Basic Formula for Pakistan

Percentage = (Obtained Value ÷ Total Value) × 100

Example for Pakistani Student: If you scored 850 marks out of 1100 in BISE Gujranwala Matric exam, your percentage = (850 ÷ 1100) × 100 = 77.27%. This is B+ Grade. Our Marks Percentage Calculator does this instantly with grade.

Example for Shopping: If a mobile price is 30,000 PKR and you want to find 15% of it (for tax), calculation = (15/100)*30,000 = 4,500 PKR.

What is a percentage calculator and how to use in Pakistan?

A percentage calculator helps you find X% of a number, e.g., 20% of 500 = 100. Formula: (Percentage/100)*Number. In Pakistan, students use it for marks (850/1100*100=77.27%), shoppers use for discount in PKR. MaazCalc provides free accurate calculation for all.

How do I calculate marks percentage for Matric and Inter in Pakistan?

For all BISE boards (Lahore, Karachi, Gujranwala, FBISE), formula is (Obtained Marks / Total Marks)*100. If you got 950 out of 1100 in Matric, percentage is (950/1100)*100=86.36% (A+ Grade). Use our dedicated Marks Percentage Calculator for Matric, Inter, O-Level, A-Level.

How do I calculate discount in PKR for Daraz shopping?

Discount Amount = Original Price * Discount% /100. Final Price = Original - Discount. Example: Original 5000 PKR, 20% off = 1000 PKR discount, final = 4000 PKR. Perfect for Daraz 11.11, Eid Sale, bazaar shopping in Pakistan. Try our Discount Calculator in PKR.

How do I calculate profit or loss % for shop in Pakistan?

Profit = Selling Price - Cost Price. Profit % = (Profit/Cost)*100. If you buy for 1000 PKR and sell for 1250 PKR, profit = 250 PKR, profit % = 25%. Loss = Cost - Selling. Useful for kirana, clothes, mobile shopkeepers and Daraz sellers. Use our Profit Loss Calculator.

How is GPA and CGPA calculated for FAST, NUST, UET in Pakistan?

GPA = Σ(Grade Point × Credit Hours) / Σ(Credit Hours) on 4.0 scale. Example: Subject1 3.7 GPA × 3 credits, Subject2 4.0 × 3 credits = (11.1+12)/6=3.85 GPA. CGPA is average of all semesters. Used in FAST, NUST, LUMS, UET, KU, PU. Our GPA CGPA Calculator supports all credit hours and converts to percentage.
